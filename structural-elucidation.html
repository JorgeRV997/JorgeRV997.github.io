Connecting experimental evidence with molecular modelling

A structure proposed from HRMS and NMR must sometimes be distinguished from several plausible regioisomers or stereoisomers. Conformational exploration and computational predictions provide independent evidence to test competing assignments against experimental observations, rather than replacing spectroscopic interpretation.

The workflow below is representative of our computational studies. Calculated NMR shifts and DP4+ can support relative stereochemical discrimination; experimental vs TD-DFT ECD spectra support absolute-configuration analysis. Measured coupling constants, NOE correlations and chemical context constrain and strengthen these assignments. Not every compound requires every technique.

Computational elucidation workflow

From molecular hypothesis to an experimentally supported stereochemical assignment.

01 / WORKFLOWExperimental constraintsHRMS formula; 1D/2D NMR; coupling constants / NOESY or ROESY.
02 / WORKFLOWMolecular modelsConstruct plausible stereoisomers and conformers in Maestro.
03 / WORKFLOWConformational searchMacroModel / Monte Carlo sampling for candidate structures.
04 / WORKFLOWFiltering & rankingRemove RMSD duplicates (e.g. 0.5 Å); rank by energy and restraints.
05 / WORKFLOWDFT geometryOptimise relevant conformers and confirm minima with frequencies.
06 / WORKFLOWSpectroscopic predictionGIAO NMR shifts / J couplings and TD-DFT ECD as applicable.
07 / WORKFLOWStatistical / ECD comparisonBoltzmann averages, DP4+ probabilities and calculated vs experimental ECD.
08 / WORKFLOWStereochemical assignmentIntegrate NMR, NOE, J and ECD evidence to assign relative / absolute configuration.

Two complementary evidence streams: chemical-shift predictions, coupling constants and DP4+ primarily inform relative stereochemistry; ECD spectra interpreted with TD-DFT assist absolute-configuration assignment. Solvent effects and conformer populations are accounted for when appropriate.

01 / JOURNAL OF NATURAL PRODUCTS · 2025 / UNIVERSITY OF GALWAY

Contignasterines A & B: unusual marine sponge steroids

Two new 2-aminoimidazole-containing steroids were characterised from Neopetrosia cf. rava. Computational conformational analysis and TD-DFT ECD calculations contributed to the stereochemical study, complementing detailed NMR/ROESY evidence. In particular, the experimental ECD spectrum of contignasterine A closely matched the calculated spectrum, supporting its absolute configuration.

SELECTED SCIENTIFIC OUTPUTS
  • Two novel steroidal metabolites, one with an unusual phosphate substituent.
  • A rare cis C/D ring junction for contignasterine A, contrasting with the trans junction in contignasterine B.
  • Combined conformational and spectroscopic evidence clarified the stereochemical analysis; the paper also reported anti-inflammatory assays.

The paper's author-contribution statement explicitly credits J. R. Virués-Segovia with performing the computational calculations alongside J. O. Vidal.

02 / MARINE DRUGS · 2023

ECD-assisted assignment of fungal eremophilanes

View full-size figure ↗

Experimental ECD curves were compared with TD-DFT spectra to support stereochemical characterization of newly reported eremophilane-type sesquiterpenes from Emericellopsis maritima. The calculated spectra complement 1D/2D NMR and other spectroscopic evidence.

METHODS IN CONTEXT
  • Conformer-based ECD calculation and spectral comparison.
  • Structural interpretation integrated with HRMS and NMR.

03 / IJMS & JMSE · 2025

Stereochemical characterization of biotransformation products

View full-size figure ↗

Stereoisomeric thiochroman sulfoxides and the new ring-opened product (S)-6a were assigned using Mosher-type NMR derivatization, experimental ECD and TD-DFT calculations. In a separate indane/phenylpropyl study, ECD and optical rotation dispersion computations supported assignments of optically active products.

SELECTED SCIENTIFIC OUTPUTS
  • Mosher analysis with computational ECD confirmation for sulfoxide stereoisomers.
  • ECD / ORD applied to indane and phenylpropyl derivatives.
  • Chiral HPLC independently quantified enantio- and diastereoselectivity.
